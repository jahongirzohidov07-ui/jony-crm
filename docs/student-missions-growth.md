# Student missions, practice choice and learning summary

Weekly life missions lead the English/German journey while preserving grammar navigation. Each mission has a concrete output, self-check criteria and a portfolio route to the corresponding week-end lesson. Mission output is practice evidence, not automatic proficiency certification.

Optional contextual preparation preserves the original graded question. Audited grammar targets offer everyday, football and robot variants plus a scaffold toggle. Local preparation never awards coins, XP or plan completion. Unsupported targets remain canonical exercises rather than guessed conversions.

The server's session-summary endpoint reports the current learner/language/saved daily plan. First checked correct responses, actual wrong-to-right corrections, remaining errors and ungraded output are separate. Comparisons use the same questions from an earlier day. Review dates come from existing scheduling. Reload does not lose evidence; missing/stale plans and insufficient history do not invent results. Evidence links open the corresponding original question.

Validation results and deployment identity are recorded in the release PR. Android tests use the real app WebView against isolated fixtures, with mocked AI; these do not establish learning impact or acoustic quality.
